Overview

Custom Domain lets creators on Stck.me use their own web address instead of the standard Stck.me link. It helps their brand, makes them look more professional and gives them more control over how they show up online.

Problem

Standard links held creators back

A shared link looks less professional

It was hard to look professional on a stck.me address.

Setup was too technical

Creators without tech skills found custom domains hard to set up and manage.

Changes were a pain

Updating things was hard, so creators' brands looked different from place to place.

The flow

From settings to a connected domain

The feature lives in settings, under Pro features. A step-by-step setup takes the creator from choosing a provider to a working domain, with values to copy and paste at each step.

What we changed

One small change cut failures

Testing showed a real problem: many people skipped step three, adding the CNAME. We gave that step its own screen, added a checkbox to confirm the DNS change and rewrote the text. Failures dropped a lot, and creators told us the clearer steps made more sense.

Reflection

Making a technical task feel normal

The win was turning a scary technical task into something normal. Next, we'll keep improving it based on what creators tell us, and support more domain providers.
