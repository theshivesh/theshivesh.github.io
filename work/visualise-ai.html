Visualise AI

Turn your words into pictures

Role

Product strategyUXVisualFE prototype

Team

Co-foundersDesignersDevelopers

Timeline

1 Month
Overview

Visualise AI turns your writing into pictures. Highlight a few lines in your post, and it makes an image that matches them. It's an easy way to show what you mean.

Problem

Adding pictures was slow and costly

Too many tools

Writers had to switch apps to make and edit images. It was slow and broke their focus.

Always uploading and downloading

Moving files back and forth kept getting in the way of writing.

Designers cost money

Small businesses and individuals often couldn't afford to hire one.

Hard for non-designers

Most people don't have design skills, and most tools are made for designers.

Goals

Write and make pictures in one place

Easy pictures

Access

Make good images from text, even if you've never designed anything.

One place to work

Flow

Make and add images inside the editor. No switching, no uploading, no downloading.

Try again

Control

If an image isn't right, make a new one from the same text instead of starting over.

How words become a picture

The flow

Adding pictures without leaving the page

Visualise AI lives in the menu that appears when you select text, so making a picture feels like part of writing. Highlight some text, tap Illustrate, and the image appears next to your words. Then add it, try again or delete it.

Reflection

Pictures as part of writing

Because it lives in the highlight menu, Visualise AI made adding pictures feel like part of writing, not a separate task. The small things made it work: a first-time tip, friendly progress messages, and add, try again or delete always close by.
