Overview

Comments lets readers reply to articles on Scroll. We wanted a place for useful, well-informed discussion that makes the news better for everyone.

Goals

Good discussion, easy to join

Good discussion

Quality

Moderators check comments, so readers share useful, well-informed views.

Easy sign-up

Onboarding

Readers can sign up and post with as little effort as possible.

Simple moderation

Trust

Moderation tools that are easy to use and keep the discussion polite.

Research

Learning from other comment tools

We looked at the comment tools already out there. We compared how much you could change and how they looked, so we knew the tradeoffs before designing our own.

Design

Where comments live

  • A button on the share bar and one below the article both open comments.
  • On phones, comments open full screen.
  • Two ways to sign up before commenting: post first and confirm your email after, or log in or sign up before you post.
How it works

How a comment goes live

Readers sign up once, with as few steps as we could manage. After that, every comment follows the same path: post, get checked, and get an email that brings the reader back.
