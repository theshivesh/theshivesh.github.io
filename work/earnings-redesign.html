Stck · 2023 to 2025

Earnings Redesign

An earnings page creators can trust

Role

Product strategyUXVisualFE prototype

Team

Co-foundersDesignersDevelopers

Timeline

2 Weeks
Overview

Creators on Stck found their earnings page hard to understand. The new design shows one month at a time, splits sales by where they came from (website, books and the Stck app), lists every fee taken out, says when the payment arrives and gives every book its own sales page.

Problem

Creators didn't trust their earnings page

Unclear totals

The current earnings weren't labelled well and had no dates attached.

No timezone

Nothing said which timezone the numbers used, so they felt unreliable.

Hidden fees

The breakdown hid fees instead of showing them clearly.

Unclear payment dates

Creators couldn't tell when they'd be paid, or how much.

Messy history

Past payments were badly organised and used different timezones.

No view per book

Website, book and app sales were mixed together, so writers couldn't see how each book was selling.

How it works

How a sale becomes a payment

Every number on the page follows the same rules. Each sale then follows the same path, so creators can track their money from the reader's payment to the day it reaches them.

Every number on the page

Then, every time

Before

The old page

The old page showed totals and readers, but left out what creators needed to trust it: dates, timezone, fees and when they'd be paid.

First version

Every fee, every week

Our first fix, in 2024, put clear dates and a UTC note on every total, listed every fee and set a weekly payment day.

Latest version

A page that explains itself

As writers started selling print books and earning in the app, one weekly total wasn't enough. The latest page shows one month at a time, splits sales by source and ends with a payment date you can plan around.

Per book

Every book gets its own page

From Insights, writers can open any post, ebook or print book and see how it's selling on its own.

A chance to grow

Clear numbers that also help sales

While working on the page, we spotted a chance to grow. Regular creators pay a 25% fee, but Pro creators pay only 10%. The note under the breakdown shows how much more they'd keep with Pro, which turns the earnings page into an honest, well-timed upgrade prompt.

Reflection

It was all about trust

We got here in two steps. The first version added dates, a UTC note and every fee. The latest one lets writers pick a month, see where every rupee came from, check each book on its own and know the payment lands on the 5th. Together they turned a confusing page into one that explains itself.
