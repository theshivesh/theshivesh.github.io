Picto AI

Make AI images right inside the editor

Team

Timeline

Overview

Picto lets writers on Stck make images with AI without leaving the editor. Type what you want to see, and Picto makes it. Use it for book covers, photos, illustrations or art.

Problem

Making images was slow and happened somewhere else

Too many steps

Making an image and adding it to a post took longer than creators wanted.

No way to use it right away

A new image couldn't go straight into a draft, a published story or a book cover.

Not in the editor

Creators had to download the image, go back to the editor and upload it again. It broke their focus.

No way to edit

Creators couldn't change anything about the image, so they lost interest.

Results weren't good enough

The images didn't make people want to keep going, so fewer people paid for premium.

Goals

Make creating images easy

Keep it simple

Clarity

A clear, friendly screen that creators understand straight away.

Make it faster

Speed

Fewer steps between an idea and a good image.

Let people edit

Control

Tools and filters so creators can make each image their own.

How it works

An image tool inside the draft

Picto opens where the writing happens. Making an image is then a short loop: pick one, edit it, place it, and start the next one in the same draft.

Reflection

Fewer choices made it better

We kept one clear text box and hid everything else under advanced options. That let new creators go from an idea to an image in their draft without leaving the page. Next, we want better models, more styles and more editing tools, based on watching how people really use it.
