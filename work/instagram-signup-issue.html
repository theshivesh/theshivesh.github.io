Stck Sign-up Fix

Fixing a broken Google sign-up

Role

Product strategyUXInvestigation

Team

Co-foundersDesignersDevelopers

Timeline

1 Week
Overview

When people tried to sign up or log in to Stck.me with Google inside Instagram's browser, it quietly failed. Instagram's browser can't handle the redirect Google sign-in needs, so people got stuck halfway with no warning or error.

Impact

We were quietly losing new users

Confused people

People got stuck when Google sign-up quietly failed inside Instagram.

Many gave up

A lot of people left because they couldn't sign in with Google.

Lost sign-ups

Every failed attempt was a new user we didn't get.

Less trust

Failing again and again with no message made Stck feel broken.

The fix

Spot it, explain it, send them on

Instagram's browser can't handle the redirect Google sign-in needs. Instead of letting it fail quietly, we spot when someone is in Instagram's browser, turn off Google sign-up with a short explanation, and give them one tap to reopen the page in their normal browser.

Reflection

Quiet failures cost the most

A tiny fix with a big effect. A few lines of code and one honest message saved sign-ups we had been quietly losing. The lesson stayed with us: the failures nobody sees are the ones that cost the most.
